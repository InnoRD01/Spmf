Good design, within reach of every Himalayan classroom.

Studio Sensorium works with schools, educators and learners across the Himalayas to make thoughtful design accessible where it matters most: in education.

What we do

We bring the care of a design studio to communities that rarely have access to one.

Design for learning

Learning materials, spaces and visual identities shaped around how students and teachers actually work.

Accessible by intent

As a non-profit, we partner with schools and organisations that would otherwise go without design support.

Rooted in the mountains

Our work starts from the places, languages and cultures of the Himalayan communities we design with.

Working in education in the Himalayas? Let's talk.

Whether you run a school, a programme or a project, we'd like to hear what you're working on.
