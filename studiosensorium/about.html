About

A design studio that works as a non-profit.

We believe good design shouldn't depend on where you live. Studio Sensorium exists to bring it to schools and learners across the Himalayas.

Our mission

Across the Himalayan region, schools and education programmes do vital work with few resources, and professional design is rarely one of them.

Studio Sensorium closes that gap. We work in the education sector as a design studio and a non-profit, so the schools, educators and organisations we partner with get careful, considered design that serves their learners.

How we work

Listen first

We start with the people in the classroom: students, teachers and the communities around them.

Design with care

We hold education work to the same standard as any studio brief.

Keep it accessible

We make design practical to use, maintain and share, long after a project ends.
